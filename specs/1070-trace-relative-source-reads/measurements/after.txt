Findings during T026:
- A library compile's temporary rmeta is renamed away (FR-010). A binary
  compile writes deps/rmeta*/full.rmeta and deletes it rather than renaming
  it, so that entry is a real short-lived write. The harness assertion was
  narrowed to library compiles.
- Every write-set entry reports survived_trace_window=true and a zero
  sha256, including deleted temporaries. This is milestone 210's
  close-time hashing, never implemented, and is filed as #1144.
- 1 harness run in 32 missed a crate root from its compile's read set.
  Exec records and file records arrive through different ring buffers, so
  a compiler starting between the two drains had its first opens processed
  before its exec, and they were dropped. Fixed by holding a
  compiler-named process's operations until its exec is processed
  (compiler_pipeline.rs `held`), with a unit test pinning the ordering.
  After the fix, 10 of 10.
